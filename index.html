The Problem

Why Current Tracking Systems Fail

Key challenges facing India's skilling ecosystem today

📉
No Employment Outcome Visibility

Training institutes and government bodies have no real-time data on where trainees end up after completion — making impact assessment impossible.

🧩
Fragmented Skill Gap Data

Skill gap information is scattered across sectors, states, and ministries with no unified view — curriculum updates lag demand by months or years.

📊
Initiative ROI is Unknown

Billions invested in PMKVY and DDU-GKY lack clear outcome measurement — placement rates, salary uplift, and time-to-hire go untracked.

🔄
Demand-Supply Mismatch

Employers cannot signal real-time skill demand, causing training programs to prepare workers for roles that no longer exist in the market.
